Ruby Games Opens Its 2026 Competitive Season

New squads, structured training and more ways for students to join the competition.

By: Ruby Games Editorial Team

Published: 12 August 2026

A New Season Begins

Ruby Games is beginning its 2026 competitive season with refreshed team rosters, weekly practice sessions and a campus championship open to student teams. Members can follow upcoming competitions through the tournament hub, where schedules, brackets and match reminders are available in one place.

Students who want to compete can explore the club roster, book a training room and take part in workshops covering communication, strategy and match preparation. The programme is designed for both experienced competitors and newcomers who want to develop their skills in a supportive team environment.

Play, improve and compete together

Our goal is to give every member a clear path from their first practice session to representing Ruby Games in competition.
